Counts are reported as identified $D$ direction / observed opposite changes in
the two assays. For each endpoint, rectangles are eligible when its saved
experimental truth is defined: nonzero interval-identified $D$ for direction,
and opposite identified assay-change directions for the joint-choice endpoint.
Unique molecular identities, chemical groups, genes and actual assay pairs are
counted directly among those eligible comparison rows. Exact rectangles have
four exact source measurements; censor-involving rectangles contain at least
one censored measurement. The prediction cohort is already restricted to the
saved common-model support. Zero-support endpoint/domain combinations remain
explicit in the full support table.

Rectangles reuse molecular and assay endpoints, so these counts describe
support rather than independent replicates. Primary macro estimates give equal
weight to defined actual assay pairs; the reported rectangle counts are not
substituted for those weights. Endpoint-specific cohorts, generalization
regimes and metadata-matched subsets can contain different molecules or assay
pairs. All counts were checked against the original compact global-baseline
context counts, using positive counts separately for each endpoint, rather than
a shared union-of-endpoints support field.
