**Paired changes in experimental decision accuracy after adding score-profile information.** Panels a,b show new molecules with known proteins; c,d show new proteins with known molecules. Left: agreement with the interval-identified direction of D, the difference between two molecular changes at distinct target assays; predicted ties receive half credit. Right: the joint choice is correct only when both predicted molecular changes have the observed opposite directions. Each point is the indicated arm minus the global-level-plus-chemistry baseline on common evaluated rectangles, in percentage points (pp); horizontal lines are the 2.5th–97.5th percentiles of 400 paired chemical-cluster resampling draws with target-assay contexts fixed. Actual assay pairs receive equal weight in the all-source operational IC50 contrast domain. Source variants retain the original identity weighting; source records and bounds are not pooled into a thermodynamic selectivity estimate. These intervals preserve molecule dependencies and are not rectangle-binomial intervals or a declared success threshold. The full-profile arm adds the full centered profile. The matching-target residual arm adds a source-matched column residual. The three plotted series use native-matched-common support within each readout: all arms and the baseline are evaluated on the same outcomes. Native scores are unfitted; the profile and residual arms are fitted readouts. Native scores are not assigned calibrated RMSE. All eight readouts are shown in their prespecified order. Boltzina shares Boltz-2 lineage, and the two GNINA readouts share a scoring architecture; these are not eight independent method replications. The source score input contains 930 identities per readout; 928 identities have eligible experimental observations. These are distinct from the 909 identities complete across all twelve channels in the descriptive geometry analysis. Actual decision support is reported separately. Native availability and resulting decision support differ between readouts; this is not a method ranking on an identical complete cohort.
