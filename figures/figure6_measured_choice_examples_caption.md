Open circles mark strict observed boundaries and arrows show the unbounded direction, not an invented point observation. Shading marks the opposing-sign quadrant identified by the measurements. Nesso documentation defines its raw affinity_pred_value as log10(IC50/μM). The cached oriented score is its negative, equal algebraically to nominal predicted pIC50 minus 6.
