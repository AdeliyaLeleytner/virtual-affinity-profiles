**When do virtual affinity profiles improve biological decisions?** The final graphical abstract shows real prepared molecules (butenafine and indomethacin, RDKit depictions of cached reference SMILES), source-deposited protein illustrations and the named molecules’ actual Docking44 score profiles and row means. The mean/residual plots illustrate the representation; they are not result-selected success cases. The experiment compares chemistry plus global score level, profile shape and query-specific scores on measured preference changes in distinct new-molecule/known-protein and new-protein/known-molecule regimes. The conclusion is scoped to the evaluated score panels and measured decision endpoints; it does not assert universal absence of useful profile information. The protein illustrations are cached smoothed C-alpha backbone traces of source-deposited DRD2/6CM4, LCK/6PDJ and AR/3B66 coordinates, using the first model’s longest chain. They are illustrative receptor examples, not new predictions or attestations that the deposited chains equal the experimental assay constructs. Deposition/retrieval provenance and chosen chains remain in assets/structure_retrieval.json and graphical_abstract_render_receipt.json. Numerical chart primitives and composition text remain vector in PDF/SVG; molecular/protein picture layers are intentionally raster illustrations. The archived graphical_abstract_draft files are preserved.
